## 1. Scope and local/demo mode

This Privacy Policy applies to the QVANTA web application and related account, circuit, simulation, learning, and AI Tutor features. It describes the baseline behavior of this product; the actual data flow depends on the deployment, enabled integrations, and configuration used by the service operator.

A local development or demo host may use a mock account, browser storage, local API, or disposable data. Local/demo mode is not a production privacy environment: data may be visible to other people who can access the machine or development services, may be reset, and may not receive production retention or deletion handling.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not enter personal, regulated, confidential, or sensitive information there.

## 2. Information QVANTA may receive

Depending on the features you use and the deployment configuration, QVANTA may receive:

- **Account information:** email address, authentication identifiers, role or plan, and account timestamps.
- **Workspace content:** circuit definitions, code, simulator settings, measurement results, notes, and other content you choose to save.
- **Tutor requests:** prompts, conversation messages, generated responses, and context you provide to the AI Tutor.
- **Technical information:** device and browser details, IP or network information available to the deployment, logs, error reports, feature usage, and security events.
- **Messages to us:** information you include when you contact the service operator or request support.

## 3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

- create and authenticate your account and keep sessions working;
- save, load, simulate, display, and troubleshoot your circuits and workspace;
- send tutor prompts and circuit context to the AI features you request and return their results;
- operate, secure, debug, measure, and improve QVANTA and its integrations;
- prevent abuse, enforce the Terms of Service, and comply with legal obligations; and
- respond to support or contact requests.

## 4. API, AI Tutor, and circuit data handling

When you run a simulation or use the AI Tutor, the request may include your circuit or code, gates and parameters, simulator settings, results, prompt or conversation text, account or session identifiers, and technical metadata. The QVANTA web client may send that information to the QVANTA API so the requested feature can run.

The API may then call an enabled AI model, quantum service, storage system, or other provider. Those providers can process the request under their own terms and privacy notices. Provider retention, model training, location, and security controls depend on the provider and configuration; this baseline does not promise that a provider will delete data immediately or never use it to improve its services. Review the provider settings before sending confidential material.

> [!CAUTION]
> QVANTA does not need you to submit secrets to teach quantum concepts. Use synthetic or non-sensitive examples whenever possible, and remove credentials, private keys, personal data, and confidential research from prompts and circuits.

## 5. Third-party services and sharing

QVANTA may share information with service providers that host the application, authenticate users, store data, provide analytics or error monitoring, or supply AI, API, and quantum features. They may process only what the configured integration needs, but their independent terms and privacy practices apply. QVANTA does not control third-party outages, security, retention, or changes.

Information may also be disclosed when needed to protect users or the service, investigate abuse, respond to lawful requests, enforce the Terms of Service, or complete a business or infrastructure change. We do not promise that every deployment has the same providers or data locations.

## 6. Storage, retention, and deletion

Account and workspace data may be stored in the deployment's database, object storage, logs, browser storage, or provider systems. We retain information for as long as needed to provide the requested feature, maintain security and reliability, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, or keep backups and operational records. Retention periods vary by deployment and provider.

You can stop sending new Content and may request account or data deletion from the service operator. A deletion request may not remove information already held in provider backups, security logs, or systems that must be retained by law. Local/demo data may instead be removed by clearing the relevant local environment.

## 7. Security and your choices

The service operator may use authentication controls, access restrictions, encrypted connections, logging, and other safeguards appropriate to its deployment. No internet service or local development environment is guaranteed to be perfectly secure, so protect your credentials and avoid sending information that requires controls QVANTA or its providers do not offer.

Depending on where you live and how the service is operated, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. You may also be able to withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. Requests and applicable exceptions are handled by the responsible operator, not by the QVANTA interface alone.

## 8. Children and sensitive information
